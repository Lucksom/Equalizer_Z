3. Acoustic Analysis by Preset
Pure Voice (BUTTON_PURE_VOICE)
 * Target: Vocal clarity, sibilance preservation, and speech intelligibility.
 * Filter Curve: A steep notch at 500\text{ Hz} (-2\text{ dB}) cleans up muddy chest resonance and acoustic cavity reflections. A wide +5\text{ dB} shelf from 2\text{ kHz} to 8\text{ kHz} targets formant frequencies F_2, F_3, and speech consonants (/s/, /t/, /k/). A high-shelf tail of +3\text{ dB} at 16\text{ kHz} preserves vocal breath and open-mic air.
Pop
 * Target: Compensates for typical V-shaped consumer tunings with high-energy bass and suppressed harsh mid-range frequencies.
 * Filter Curve: Mild low-end lift (+2\text{ dB} to +3\text{ dB}), steep scoop across lower-mids (500\text{ Hz} at -6\text{ dB}, 1\text{ kHz} at -5\text{ dB}), with controlled upper treble (+2\text{ dB} at 8\text{ kHz}) to eliminate fatigue during extended listening.
Rock
 * Target: Punchy kick drums, aggressive electric guitar bite, and bright cymbals.
 * Filter Curve: Consistent +3\text{ dB} boost from sub-bass to upper-bass (31.25\text{ Hz} to 250\text{ Hz}), a minor mid dip at 500\text{ Hz} (-2\text{ dB}) to decouple bass lines from guitars, followed by an aggressive upward ramp hitting +5\text{ dB} at 4\text{ kHz} and +6\text{ dB} at 8\text{ kHz}\text{--}16\text{ kHz}.
Jazz
 * Target: Warm acoustic instrumentation, upright double-bass presence, and intimate saxophone/brass bodies.
 * Filter Curve: Low-end remains completely neutral (0\text{ dB} up to 125\text{ Hz}). Lower mids (250\text{ Hz} to 1\text{ kHz}) are boosted by +5\text{ dB} to fill out horn timbres and snare body, balanced with open air at 16\text{ kHz} (+5\text{ dB}).
Classical
 * Target: Natural orchestral dynamics and high-frequency roll-off to emulate concert-hall acoustic absorption.
 * Filter Curve: Perfectly flat response through 1\text{ kHz}, followed by aggressive high-frequency damping: -5\text{ dB} across 2\text{ kHz}\text{--}8\text{ kHz}, descending to -8\text{ dB} at 16\text{ kHz} to suppress string shrillness.

6. Power Bass: Bass Boost Model
Selecting Power Bass (BUTTON_BASS = 1) in ControlPanelEffect.java executes:
BassBoostMz bassBoostEffect = getBassBoostEffect(audioSession);
bassBoostEffect.setEnabled(true);
bassBoostEffect.setStrength((short) 1000);

Coupled with EQ_BASS_DEFAULT = "19181614101010101010", this preset implements a two-stage low-frequency reinforcement pipeline:
 * Dynamic Psychoacoustic Harmonics: A non-linear filter inside libbassboostMz.so generates missing fundamental harmonics (2nd and 3rd harmonics) at 100% processing strength (1000 / 1000).
 * Fixed Low-Shelf Boost: Applies static linear post-filtering:
   * +9.0\text{ dB} at 31.25\text{ Hz}
   * +8.0\text{ dB} at 62.5\text{ Hz}
   * +6.0\text{ dB} at 125\text{ Hz}
   * +4.0\text{ dB} at 250\text{ Hz}

// Map Meizu Square Sound modes into DynamicsProcessing stages
fun applyMeizuPreset(dp: DynamicsProcessing, virt: Virtualizer?, mode: MeizuMode) {
    when (mode) {
        MeizuMode.EFFECT_3D -> {
            virt?.apply {
                enabled = true
                if (strengthSupported) setStrength(650.toShort())
                forceVirtualizationMode(Virtualizer.VIRTUALIZATION_MODE_BINAURAL)
            }
            // Set linear flat response on DynamicsProcessing stages
            for (i in 0 until 10) {
                dp.setPostEqBandAllChannelsTo(i, DynamicsProcessing.EqBand(true, FREQ[i], 0.0f))
            }
        }
        MeizuMode.PURE_VOICE -> {
